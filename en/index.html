The decisive moment

The cost of an error
depends on when
you find it.

Everything is decided at the design stage. The shape given to a solution sets its production cost, and the cost of its entire life cycle, long before the first unit.

The cost of the same error, depending on when it is caught

$1 caught at the design stage
$10 caught at the prototype stage
$100 caught once the product is on the market

Rixid works at the first stage. Anticipating the problem and solving it while it still costs a dollar: before it reaches the field, before the recall, before it is too late to fix.

The 1-10-100 rule, formalized by Labovitz and Chang, Making Quality Work (1992).
